Rules used

K-factor: 40 · Default starting Elo: 1,000 · Format: FT4.

Expected score uses the standard Elo formula. The score multiplier is calibrated so an equal-rated 4–3 win gives exactly 16 regular Elo points: M = 0.8 + 0.2 × (4 − losing rounds) / 3.

The winning team's streak increments before its bonus is calculated: first consecutive win +1, second +2, third +3, fourth +4, fifth and later +5. The losing team's streak resets to zero. The winner's streak bonus is not deducted from the loser.

Because the streak bonus is added only to the winner, the system is intentionally not zero-sum.
